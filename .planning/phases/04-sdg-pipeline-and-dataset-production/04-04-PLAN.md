<objective>
Create the OSFT JSONL serializer and validator tools that convert sdg_hub traces into Training Hub's expected format (D-18, D-20) and verify schema compliance (D-04).

Purpose: sdg_hub produces raw tool-calling traces as DataFrames. These must be converted to OSFT-compatible JSONL with proper messages arrays, tool_calls, and tool_call_id threading. The validator ensures every output line meets the schema before delivery. Both single-turn and multi-turn trajectory formats are produced (D-20).
Output: serialize-osft.py (serializer with --format single-turn|multi-turn|both), validate-osft-schema.py (validator), and tests for both.
</objective>
